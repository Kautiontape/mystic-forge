Mana Pool prices, and a year of history instead of 90 days

Mana Pool joins TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, and Cardmarket, so every watched card carries a fourth price. Watchlists also keep more history than MTGJSON hands out: prices now land in a store the server maintains itself, at daily resolution for 120 days and weekly averages kept indefinitely after that. A list you have held for a year shows a year rather than the rolling ~90 days MTGJSON still has. Adding a card used to mean waiting while a 1.4 GB price file streamed end to end just to find that card's rows; it now reads the store by index, so the history is there before the page finishes loading. The MTGStocks badge on a shared list points at a real page now instead of a 404.
