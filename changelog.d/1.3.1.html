Boards open in a blink again

Every board took three to four seconds to open, and a little longer each day. The line at the bottom that says which date the prices run through was to blame: answering it meant reading every price on record for the shops on the board — a million and a half rows, and growing with each nightly ingest. The database now keeps an index shaped for exactly that question, and the same line resolves in three lookups. A side effect worth having: link previews in Slack, Discord and the like had started giving up before the page arrived and falling back to a bare domain name; they get the board's title and banner again.
