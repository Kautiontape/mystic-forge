The health check stops recounting price history

The status page at /mtg/health reports how much price history the server keeps: about 74 million prices in a 1.35 GB file. It used to count them every time it was asked, and the server's own container check asks every minute, so the count ran about every two minutes. The machine Mystic Forge runs on can't hold that file in memory, so each count read about 2 GB from disk, and together they made up most of the machine's disk reads. On October 28 the machine stalled twice, for one to three minutes each time, while its disk was saturated, and requests went unanswered while it lasted. The counts are now taken once, right after the nightly price update, and stored. The status page reports the stored figures along with the time they were taken. Tools, watchlists and price history are unchanged.
