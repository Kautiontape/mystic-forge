Every market at once, targets that move, and lists that travel

The watchlist board now compares all four price sources. The prices dropdown opens on All markets, where each card shows the cheapest of TCGplayer, Card Kingdom and Mana Pool and names which one it is; pick a single market to see everything at that shop instead (Cardmarket stays in euro and is never mixed into the cheapest). A card can also be pinned to one market for good, from its own modal, when that is the only place you would buy it — targets, the board and the push alerts all judge the same basis, so a lit card and a notification never disagree.

Targets learned to move. Besides a fixed number, a card can follow the historic low: the target sits at the lowest price recorded so far (or a chosen percentage under it) and shifts whenever a new low is set, so a buy window opens only on a genuine dip. The modal offers gg.deals-style shortcuts — beat the current price, match the low, ten, twenty or thirty percent under either — and the same grammar works everywhere a target is typed: @ low, @ low-10%, @ -20% on a line, or the new target_rule and shop inputs on watchlist_add and watchlist_bulk_add.

The card modal was rebuilt around what you do there: the card face beside four figures (now, lowest, target, thirty-day change), a market row that flips the chart between shops, the target editor, hop-outs that now include Card Kingdom and Mana Pool, and the daily table folded away under Recent prices. Remove sits far left, Save and Bought far right. The BUY/WAIT banner above the board is gone.

Lists travel. Export writes plain 1 Card Name lines for everything, for the cards at target, or for a hand-picked set, and opens them in TCGplayer Mass Entry or the Card Kingdom deck builder, or copies them for Mana Pool. Import takes the same lines back, with targets after @ and an optional pin to the printing each line names. And a list no longer needs an assistant to exist: /mtg/w/new forges one by hand from a name and a pasted list, and hands over the passphrase once.

The board compares all four markets at once and lets you pin a card to one; targets can follow the historic low instead of sitting still; the card modal shows the card face, per-market prices and a proper target editor; lists export straight into TCGplayer, Card Kingdom or Mana Pool and import back; and /mtg/w/new forges a list by hand, no assistant needed.
