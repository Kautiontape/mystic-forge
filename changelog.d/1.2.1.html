The new price store now actually delivers

A fast follow to this morning's release. The price store was built and full, but two delivery gaps kept its contents from reaching existing watchlists: a card that already had prices from the original three shops was never handed Mana Pool's history — the backfill only asked "does this card have any prices?", the wrong question the moment a fourth source exists — and a deploy landing after that day's ingest had already run meant new ingest work, like the MTGStocks link cache, silently waited until tomorrow. Both fixed: the server now re-delivers the store's full history whenever the store is rebuilt or gains a price source, and a version change forces one ingest immediately instead of trusting yesterday's date stamp.
